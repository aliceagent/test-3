# Competitive Landscape Report

## Structure (per the NewVoices_Competitor_DeepDive format)
1. **"About this report"** preamble: which companies, why chosen, and an explicit sourcing note.
2. **Tiering**: group competitors by strategic position (closest rivals / adjacent generalists / enterprise incumbents pressing from above / vertical specialists), not alphabetically.
3. **At-a-glance table**: Company | Tier | Entry pricing | Headline metric.
4. **Per-company profile**: What it is / Founded, HQ, founders / Funding / Compliance → Business overview → Success metrics → Pricing → Onboarding (what a new user actually sees, with URLs) → Strengths & weaknesses (candid).
5. **Positioning conclusion**: where the open gap is and how the client fills it.

## Sourcing rules
- Pricing: from live pricing pages, verified in-browser, dated ("as of <month year>").
- Funding/ARR/valuations: cite source (Crunchbase, press, Latka, G2/Trustpilot).
- Mark every self-reported, stale, or contested number with a ⚠ caution flag.
- Vendor case-study claims labeled as such, never presented as independent fact.
- If screenshots can't be captured, document flows in words + direct links and say so explicitly.

## Angles to always cover
- B2B and B2C perspectives when the product straddles both.
- Pricing-model differences (per-minute vs. seat vs. outcome-based) as a strategic signal.
- Onboarding friction as a differentiator, not just features.
