# Pitch Deck

## Process
1. **Identify the audience first**: investor (raise), enterprise buyer (sale), or internal leadership (socialization). If not stated, infer from content and say which you assumed.
2. **Extract the narrative** before designing: Problem → Why now → Solution → Proof/demo → Market → Competition → Business model → Team → Ask. Cut sections that don't apply; never pad.
3. Build as an HTML/design artifact unless a .pptx is explicitly requested (decks get exported to PDF for sharing).

## House style (from NewVoices/Equity Earn decks)
- Section kickers in spaced small caps ("T H E  P R O B L E M" style), one idea per slide.
- Numbered slide footers, "CONFIDENTIAL · INTERNAL" tags on internal decks.
- Concrete proof over adjectives: live-demo callouts, real metrics, named clients.
- Competition slides as a table: Category / Who's there / What they do / The gap — ending with "Our position".
- Financial slides show the math (funnel assumptions, unit economics), not just totals.
- Dark, premium visual treatment for finance/enterprise; keep text high-contrast and scannable.

## Recurring gotchas
- Slides must never cut off content — verify every slide renders fully (a past complaint: "the first part, it's cut in half").
- Corrected facts from prior iterations (dates, birth years, valuations) must carry forward — re-read the latest source, not the first draft.
- When updating "according to boss's instructions", enumerate each instruction and mark it applied, so nothing is silently skipped.
